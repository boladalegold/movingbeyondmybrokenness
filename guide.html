You did not cause this. And you don’t have to carry it alone.

If your teenager is struggling with anxiety or depression, you already know the exhaustion that is hard to put into words. You lie awake listening for them. You read the mood of your house before you have had your coffee. You replay every conversation and wonder, What did I do wrong?

Anchoring the Storm was written for you: the parent who loves deeply, is trying hard, and is running on empty. It is a complete training program that helps you understand what your teen is facing, steady yourself first, and become the calm, informed anchor your family needs.

Three pillars in every module

The psychological

What the research says is happening in your teen’s brain, and in yours.

The practical

Step-by-step tools and word-for-word scripts you can use tonight.

The spiritual

Prayers, lament, and faith-grounded encouragement for the long road.

What you’ll learn

Eight modules across two parts. Each can stand alone, so you can begin with whatever feels most urgent.

  1. Understanding What Your Teen Is FacingHow the adolescent brain works, the difference between sadness and depression, how anxiety hides behind “attitude,” and the warning signs that need professional help right away.
  2. The Parent’s Inner WorldRecognizing compassion fatigue, naming the grief no one talks about, and a personal first-aid kit for the moments you feel triggered.
  3. Communication That HealsWhat to say, what not to say, and the PACE approach that helps teens open up instead of shutting down.
  4. Setting Limits Without Losing the RelationshipHow to hold loving, firm boundaries with the LEAN method, with scripts like “I love you too much to let this one go.”
  5. Caring for Yourself Without GuiltWhy your renewal is one of the most strategic investments in your teen’s recovery, and a weekly plan to make it happen.
  6. Navigating the Mental Health SystemTypes of treatment and care, questions to ask providers and prescribers, and how to advocate for your teen at school.
  7. Spiritual Anchors for the Long JourneyHolding on to faith when the storm doesn’t pass as quickly as you prayed, including how to write your own prayer of lament.
  8. Building a Family That Heals TogetherCreating a home where recovery can happen, caring for siblings and your marriage, and a vision for your family beyond this season.

Created by a licensed clinician

Boladale Gold, MA, LCPC, has worked in mental health for more than fifteen years, beginning with teenagers in residential treatment. There she saw how deeply a parent’s own wellbeing shapes a child’s recovery, and how healing in one generation opens the door for the next.

She leads Divine Touch Therapeutic Counseling and Mentoring Services and Moving Beyond My Brokenness International, bringing clinical care and faith to the same table.
