Meet Boladale Gold

MA, LCPC · Founder, Moving Beyond My Brokenness International

“People cannot thrive when they cannot move beyond what broke them. The fear of the past does not get to define your future.”

Boladale is a licensed clinician who has worked in mental health since 2009, beginning with teenagers in residential treatment. There she saw how a parent’s untreated anxiety and depression can feed a child’s, and how healing in one generation opens the door for the next.

Today she leads Divine Touch Therapeutic Counseling and Mentoring Services, offering individual therapy for trauma, anxiety, mood, and work stress, and Moving Beyond My Brokenness International, a faith-based ministry that brings that same care to anyone, free of charge.
